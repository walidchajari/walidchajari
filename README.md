## About Me

I hold a Master's in Finance & Data Science and specialize in **Machine Learning applied to financial fraud detection**. My end-of-studies project (PFE) at **Peaqock Financials** is an explainable AI system that detects fraud on **ISO 20022 PACS.008** banking messages, combining Isolation Forest, GRU Autoencoders and SHAP for interpretability — deployed as a full-stack app with FastAPI, React and PostgreSQL.

I like working across the full pipeline: data ingestion and feature engineering, model design, explainability, and shipping the result as a real, usable product rather than a notebook.

- 🔍 **Focus:** Fraud detection, anomaly detection, explainable AI (XAI)
- 🧠 **Deep-diving into:** Deep Learning (GRU/Autoencoders), model interpretability (SHAP), MLOps & Docker
- 🎓 **Studying:** Master Finance & Data Science, Université Hassan II de Casablanca
- 📍 **Based in:** Casablanca, Morocco
- 💬 **Languages:** Arabic (native) · French (fluent) · English (intermediate) · German (basics)
